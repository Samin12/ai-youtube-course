# The onboarding questions

These are all the questions Claude asks you during onboarding. Here they are in one place, so you can see the whole thing up front.

**Prefer a visual?** Open [`questions.html`](questions.html) in your browser for a printable checklist. Claude opens it for you when onboarding starts.

## The fastest way: just talk

You don't have to answer these one by one. The fastest way is to read through the list, then hit record and just talk.

1. **Read the list below once,** so you know what's coming.
2. **Turn on voice.** Use the microphone button in Claude, or your computer's built-in dictation:
   - **Mac:** press the **Fn** (🌐) key twice.
   - **Windows:** press **Windows key + H**.
   - Or record a voice memo on your phone, get the transcript, and paste it in.
3. **Talk through everything in one go.** Go in any order. Ramble, jump around, repeat yourself. Messy is fine, and more is better. Talk like you're explaining your business to a friend over coffee.
4. **Send it.** Claude sorts everything into your files, shows you what it got, and only asks about what's missing or unclear.

**Tip:** do Part 1 and Part 2 in one go, or one part at a time if that's easier. You don't need to answer Part 3. Claude drafts it for you.

**At the end,** Claude builds you a one-page visual of your viewer: who they are, why they watch, why they buy, and their journey from where they are to where they want to be.

**Bonus:** the way you talk in your voice dump is the best sample of your voice there is. Claude uses it to start your brand voice guide.

## Part 1: You (the audit)

*Your "you are here" dot. Saves to `my-channel/about-me.md` and `my-channel/offer.md`.*

- **Who you are**
  - What's your name, and what do you do, in one line?
  - Do you have a YouTube channel yet? What's it called?
  - Where are you based?
- **Your story**
  - What's your background? Jobs, experience, what you've worked on.
  - What were the turning points that got you here?
- **Your business and offer**
  - What do you sell?
  - Who is it for?
  - What problem does it solve?
  - Roughly what does it cost?
  - Where do people buy or book?
  - Sell more than one thing? Which one is the main offer you want every video to point to?
  - No offer yet? That's fine. What can you do, and what cool things have you done?
- **Skill check**
  - What do people ask you about?
  - What do people pay you for?
  - What do you know better than most people around you?
- **Proof**
  - What results have you gotten for yourself? (Numbers, before and after, wins.)
  - What results have you gotten for other people? (Client wins, testimonials.)
- **What you'll make**
  - What could you make 100 videos about without getting bored?
  - What won't you make? (Topics, formats, showing your face or not.)

## Part 2: Your viewer (the viewer avatar)

*The one person you make videos for, who's also your customer. Based on Paddy Galloway's audience overview: Cluster, TAM and Personality. Saves to `my-channel/viewer-avatar.md` (why they watch) and `my-channel/customer-avatar.md` (why they buy).*

- **Got material? Paste it in first.** It makes your avatar much better.
  - Have customers? Sales-call transcripts or customer emails.
  - Starting from scratch? Comments, Reddit threads, or competitor videos.
- **If you already have customers,** describe your best current customers. They're your viewer.
- **Cluster** (1–2 words each)
  - What's your niche?
  - Roughly how old are they?
  - What countries are most of them in?
  - What channels like yours do they already watch?
- **TAM** (how big the audience is; a rough guess is fine, and Claude helps you estimate it)
  - Roughly how many people could want what you make?
  - What are your average views per video right now? (New channel? It's 0.)
- **Personality** (1–2 words each; skip any that don't help)
  - Who are they? (Job, life situation.)
  - What are they like?
  - What do they like, and what do they not like?
  - What do they come to you for?
  - What vibe do they have?
- **Problems and goals**
  - Where are they stuck?
  - What have they already tried?
  - What words do they use to describe their problem? (Their words, not yours.)
  - What would they benefit from knowing?
- **Where they hang out**
  - Online?
  - Offline?
- **The transformation**
  - Where are they now?
  - Where do they want to be?
- **Why they buy** (your customer avatar; saves to `my-channel/customer-avatar.md`; no offer yet? skip this)
  - What makes someone ready to buy from you?
  - What have they usually watched or tried before they buy?
  - What are their biggest worries before buying, and how do you answer them?
  - What do they get when they buy?
  - What does success look like after they buy? Their first win, and 90 days later?
  - Got DMs, sales calls or testimonials from buyers? Paste them in.

## Part 3: Your lane (the overlap)

*Where what you know meets what your viewer needs. Saves to `my-channel/lane.md`.*

- **You don't need to answer anything here.** Claude drafts your lane from Parts 1 and 2:
  - Your lane in one sentence: "I help [my viewer] go from [where they are now] to [where they want to be], using [what I know]."
  - 3 to 5 lane topics, each with why your viewer cares, why you're credible, and how it leads to your offer.
  - A short "not in my lane" list.
- **Then you react:**
  - Which topics feel most like you?
  - What's missing?
  - What should we cut?

## Part 4: Your voice (optional)

*How you sound, so AI writes like you. Saves to `my-channel/brand-voice.md`.*

- If you did a voice dump, Claude already has a great sample. You can skip this part.
- Otherwise:
  - How would a friend describe the way you talk?
  - What words or phrases do you use a lot? Any words you'd never use?
  - How do you like to explain things? (For example, a story or example first, then the point.)
  - Got a post, an email, or a video transcript that sounds like you? Paste it in.
