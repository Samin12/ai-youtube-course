# Videos

**This folder is where your videos live, one folder per video.** Together with `my-channel/` and `CLAUDE.md`, it's the operating system for your content.

## Start a new video

Open this course folder in Claude (the Code tab in the desktop app) and say:

> **Make a new video.**

Claude will:

1. Make the next numbered folder, like `videos/001-my-first-video/`, from [`_template/`](_template/).
2. Fill in the brief: who it's for, the problem it solves, the lane topic, and how it leads to your offer, all from your `my-channel/` files.
3. Show you the plan (Ideation → Research → Packaging → Script → Film → Edit → Repurpose → Publish) and start Stage 1 with you.

## Pick up where you left off

Say **"continue my video."** Claude opens your current video, finds the next unticked stage, and keeps going.

Say **"show my videos"** to see every video and where each one is.

## What's in each video folder

- `README.md`: the brief and the plan, with a checkbox per stage
- `01-idea.md` to `08-publish.md`: one file per stage
- `assets/`: raw footage, screen recordings, b-roll
- `exports/`: the final video, thumbnails, shorts

Big video files stay on your computer. They're not uploaded to GitHub.
