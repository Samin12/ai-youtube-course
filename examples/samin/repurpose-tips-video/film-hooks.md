# Film hooks: teleprompter checklist

Record these 15 lines in one sitting. Save each take as `hooks-filmed/<file>` (in `tips Agent/reels/hooks-filmed/`). `.mov` or `.mp4` both work. Once a file is there, `make_reel.py` renders that hook automatically.

Frame: vertical-friendly. You end up in the BOTTOM part of a 9:16 reel (1080x1110), so keep your head and hands in the middle of the frame, same camera, mic and shirt as the long video. Leave 1 s of silence before and after each line.

## Batch filming tips

1. **Same setup, one session.** Same camera spot, mic, light and shirt as the tips video, so the hook cuts into the footage without a jump. Do all 15 in one go (about 20 minutes).
2. **Three takes, three energies.** For every line do one calm, one punchy, one fast take, back to back. Clap once between lines so they're easy to find. Keep the best take and name it with the file name below.
3. **Look into the lens and start mid-energy.** No 'hey guys', no breath before the first word. The first frame is the hook: start with the gesture already moving, and stop dead on the last word (no trailing smile or 'um').
