# Idea doc: The self-updating AI trader (Claude + Hermes + Quiver)

- **Topic:** an AI trader that follows what Congress, insiders and hedge funds buy, trades on its own, and gets smarter every week
- **Idea:** connect Claude to Quiver with the new one-click connector, link a paper broker, then move the brain into Hermes so it trades without me, reviews its own trades, and rewrites its strategy
- **Unique angle**
  - The catch nobody explains: in the Claude app, every trade waits for you to tap Confirm. Claude won't trade on its own
  - The fix: Hermes, an open-source agent on a server, runs 24/7 with Claude as the brain, and it's allowed to act
  - Self-updating: every week it reviews its own trades and rewrites its strategy
  - Brand new: Quiver's one-click connector in Claude's directory [verify nobody's shown it yet]
  - Season two of my 1.8M video, plus my Hermes course (368K)
  - Inside view: I worked at JPMorgan and saw how institutional trading works

- **Why it'll work:** my own proof on both halves (1.8M and 247K on trading, 368K on Hermes), a self-improving trading agent outlier elsewhere (489K), and a new launch to ride

- **Outline**
  - **Intro**
    - Claude can now see what Congress, insiders and hedge funds are buying, place the trade, and with one more piece, do it all on its own while you sleep
    - My first video on this got 1.8M views. [My bot's been running X days]
    - 3 levels: connect it, place the trades, make it run itself
  - **Why this matters**
    - Data: they see where the money moves first (poker where the other guy sees every card)
    - Execution: they have systems, you have a job (the perfect trade, gone while you're at lunch)
    - Learning: they review every trade, most people repeat mistakes (an athlete watching game tape)
  - **Level 1: Connect Claude to Quiver and get the data**
    - One click from Claude's connector directory, then "Do you have access to Quiver?"
    - Ask what Congress, insiders and hedge funds bought in the last 30 days
    - Turn it into rules: STRATEGY.md
  - **Level 2: Place the trades**
    - Link a broker in paper mode (Liquid or Alpaca), since Quiver is the data, not the broker
    - Claude proposes a trade from the rules
    - The catch: the Confirm card. Every trade waits for you
  - **Level 3: Let Hermes run it on its own**
    - Hermes on a server (Hostinger), Claude as the brain, Telegram for updates
    - Same connections and rules, with limits: max per trade, stop-loss, paper first
    - Every week it reviews its trades and rewrites STRATEGY.md (show v1 vs v2)
    - You stay in control: limits, pause, revoke anytime
