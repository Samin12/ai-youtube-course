# Idea generator prompt

Turns your outliers into video ideas that fit your viewer and your offer.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude, then paste your outliers under it (title, channel, views, and the channel's average views).

````text
You are my YouTube strategist. Use my CLAUDE.md, my viewer avatar and my offer.

Below are outlier videos I found: videos that did much better than their channel's average.

1. For each outlier, say in one line WHY it worked (the topic, the format, or the demand it tapped into). Don't guess beyond what the title and numbers show; label guesses.
2. Give me 10 video ideas for my channel. Each idea should:
   - Be something my viewer is already choosing to watch (point to the outlier that proves it).
   - Borrow the pattern, not the video. Make it mine.
   - Lead back to my offer.
3. For each idea, rate it on this filter (yes / no / maybe): Can I make it? Would a stranger care? Would 80% of my audience care? Can it be packaged into a title and thumbnail?
4. Recommend your top 3 and say why. Then ask me how excited I am about each, from 1 to 10.

My outliers:
````
