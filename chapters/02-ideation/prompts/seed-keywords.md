# Seed keywords prompt

From Jake Trinder: ask AI for a core seed list of keywords, then search each one on YouTube and follow the outliers.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude (it should see your `CLAUDE.md` and viewer avatar). Then search each keyword on YouTube.

````text
Give me a core seed list of keywords for this niche.

Use my CLAUDE.md and viewer avatar: think about the problems my viewer searches for, in their own words.
Give me 15–20 short keywords or phrases, grouped by problem.
For each group, add one line on why my viewer would search it.
````

**Next:** search each keyword on YouTube. For each one, note the floor (what a decent video gets) and the ceiling (what the best one gets). Click into any video that beat its channel's average by a lot. That's an outlier.
