# Audience problems prompt

From Nate Curtiss's course.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude (it should see your `CLAUDE.md` and viewer avatar).

````text
What are five problems I can solve for this audience and how big is this audience?

Use my CLAUDE.md and viewer avatar. For each problem:
- Say it in my viewer's own words.
- Say how it connects to my offer.
- Give a rough sense of how many people have it, and label it a guess.
````
