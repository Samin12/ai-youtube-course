# Read my results prompt

Helps you read your YouTube analytics and decide what to change next time. Numbers "tell you where to look," not what to do. *(Jake Trinder)*

**How to use it:** after the first day, take screenshots in YouTube Studio: the overview, reach (impressions and click-through rate), engagement (retention graph and average view duration), and a few comments. Paste them into Claude with this prompt.

````text
Help me read my results for this video. Use my CLAUDE.md, viewer avatar and offer.

My screenshots are attached. Here's what I know: [how long it's been live, and how my other videos usually do].

Diagnose in this order (Aprilynne Alter's method):
1. CLICK PROBLEM? Look at impressions and click-through rate (the share of people who saw it and clicked). Was it the title, the thumbnail, or the topic?
2. WATCH PROBLEM? Look at retention at 30 seconds (Nate Curtiss's target is 60–70%), average view duration compared with my other videos, and any dips in the retention graph. Point to the moment and guess whether it's a script, filming or edit problem.
3. IDEA PROBLEM? If clicks and retention look fine but it still didn't grow, was the idea the issue?

Then:
- Read the comments. Are they positive compared with the views? (Jake Trinder calls this the best early signal.)
- Tell me if this is a video to repackage (new title and thumbnail) instead of giving up on it.
- Give me ONE thing to change in my next video, and ONE thing to keep.

Only use what's in my screenshots. If something isn't shown, say so.
````
