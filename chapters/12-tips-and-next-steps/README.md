**Tips from the research:**

- **Plan more than you produce.** Spend 10x more time on the idea, title and thumbnail than you think. *(Ed Lawrence)*
- **Treasure every view.** Channels with 400-view videos have landed huge clients. *(Ed Lawrence)* And "your highest viewed video can be one of your least valuable." *(Jake Trinder)*
- **Repeat your hits, slightly different.** *(Ed Lawrence)*
- **Mix fast and slow content.** News and trend videos grow fast; evergreen videos (ones that stay useful for years) compound. *(Ed Lawrence)*
- **Don't spread yourself too thin.** Pick one long-form and one short-form platform first. *(Ed Lawrence, Taki Moore)*
- **Give it time.** Think in 5-year timescales; 99% of people quit. *(Ed Lawrence)* Don't expect results in 3 months. Give a lane at least 10 focused videos before switching. *(Aprilynne Alter)*
- **Each video: better or different.** *(Aprilynne Alter)*
- **Batch your work.** Film 3–4 scripts in one setup. Block 1–2 hours a day. *(Ed Lawrence)*
- **Find creator friends.** A weekly mastermind with people at your commitment level. *(Aprilynne Alter)*
- **Ignore haters.** *(Ed Lawrence)*
