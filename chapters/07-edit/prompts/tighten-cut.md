# Tighten the cut prompt

Finds what to cut: mistakes, pauses, and lines that don't change anything. *(The anti-edit: Ed Lawrence. Cut where you get bored: Jon Dorman.)*

**How to use it:** get a transcript of your raw recording (with timestamps if you can) and paste it under this prompt.

````text
Below is the transcript of my raw recording. Help me cut it tight.

1. MISTAKES AND RETAKES: list every flubbed line, false start, and repeated take. Say which take to keep (usually the last clean one).
2. LINES THAT DON'T CHANGE ANYTHING: list lines I can cut without losing the point. Say why in a few words.
3. SLOW SPOTS: where would a viewer get bored? Suggest what to cut or tighten.
4. PUNCH-INS: suggest 3–5 moments for a punch-in (a quick zoom) for emphasis, at the end of a sentence.
5. CHAPTERS: suggest YouTube chapters with timestamps and short titles.

Use timestamps if the transcript has them. Don't rewrite my words; just tell me what to cut.

My transcript:
````
