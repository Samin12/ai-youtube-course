# Chapter 10: The YouTube funnel and content web

**After the 8 stages.** Views are great, but you're running a business. Let's turn those views into customers.

## Why this matters

You're not a YouTuber. You're a business owner using YouTube to grow your business. ("I'm a coach who's using YouTube to grow their business." *Taki Moore*) Most people miss one thing: the bridge from viewer to customer. This chapter builds it.

## What you'll learn

- **Two islands.** Your audience is on one island, watching your videos. You and your offer are on another. "The problem is that there's no bridge between the two." So "build the bridge first and the audience second." *(Taki Moore)*
- **Cold, warm, hot.** Cold people are skeptical. Warm people are curious. Hot people are ready. Mistake 1: treating everyone as hot (hard selling). Mistake 2: treating everyone as cold (nurturing forever). "A funnel is just a series of easy yeses." *(Taki Moore)*
- **The System.** *(Taki Moore)* *[Diagram: coming soon]* Four columns, left to right, as people move from cold to warm to hot:
  - **Lead gen:** short-form, micro magnets, a monthly workshop.
  - **Lead nurture:** chat, email, long-form, and the "Microwave" (a VSL, or video sales letter: one video that walks through your system). "This is where YouTube predominantly fits… middle of funnel, not top of funnel."
  - **Conversion:** an offer doc or a sales call.
  - **Delivery:** a short program, or monthly recurring.
  - You build it right to left: start from the product.
- **Binges are buyers.** Every buyer in two years had watched at least 47 minutes of his content, and binge viewers bought 2x. "Time on brand, not time on list." *(Taki Moore)* This ties back to the 7-11-4 rule of thumb.
- **The octopus.** *(Taki Moore)* *[Diagram: coming soon]* Picture an octopus. The body in the middle is one video that explains your offer (the Microwave). Every other video is a tentacle whose job is to bring people back to the middle. "All roads lead to Rome." Under $1M a year? One product, not five.
- **The easy yeses, step by step.** *(Taki Moore)*
  - **Micro magnet:** a quick win in 2–3 minutes, "a tool, not a teach," like a free AI tool or GPT.
  - **The Microwave:** the VSL on YouTube that takes people "warm to hot."
  - **The offer doc:** so clear you could "slide it across a desk without saying a word."
  - Don't put the whole offer in the video: "We invite people to take step one or step two, and we let step two sell three."
- **CTAs that work.** A call to action should be "a give, not a take." "The video does not have to make the whole sale, it just has to make the next useful step obvious." *(Jake Trinder)* The best on-YouTube CTA is "watch this next video." *(Rene Ritchie, in Aprilynne Alter's course)*
- **Find the bottleneck.** "Views, leads, qualified calls, customers, revenue, that's the funnel." After each video, find the weak step: not enough traffic, a bad CTA, or a funnel that leaks. *(Jake Trinder)*
- **The content web.** "Much less like a funnel and much more like a web… They find you somewhere, they explore everywhere, and then when they're ready, they make a purchase." *(Taki Moore)* Every platform you repurpose to is another strand.
- **Email.** It's "the best channel you've got because everything else is on rented platforms." *(Taki Moore)* Start a list early with a lead magnet, even before you know how you'll use it. Her coaching launch was "a six-figure launch on a brand new offer," mostly from email. *(Aprilynne Alter)*

## Steps

1. **The 60-second test.** Set a timer for 60 seconds and draw your path from "I don't know you" to "happy client paying you money." Almost nobody has a simple one. *(Taki Moore)*
2. **Map it, right to left.** Run [`prompts/funnel-map.md`](prompts/funnel-map.md). Start from your offer, then work back to the first video.
3. **Pick your micro magnet.** Run [`prompts/micro-magnet-ideas.md`](prompts/micro-magnet-ideas.md).
4. **Fill in your funnel map** in `my-channel/funnel-map.md`.
5. **Fix your CTAs.** Make sure every video's call to action points to the same next step.

## Tips

- **One product, not five,** until you're past $1M a year. *(Taki Moore)*
- **Start your email list early.** *(Aprilynne Alter)*
- **Small CTAs show up earlier too:** in your packaging, your script's ending, and your description's first line.
