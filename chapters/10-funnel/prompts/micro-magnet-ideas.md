# Micro magnet ideas prompt

A micro magnet is a quick win in 2–3 minutes: "a tool, not a teach," like a free AI tool or GPT. *(Taki Moore)*

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude. It should see your `CLAUDE.md`, viewer avatar and offer.

````text
Give me 5 micro magnet ideas for my viewer.

A micro magnet is a quick win my viewer can get in 2–3 minutes. It's a tool, not a teach: something they use (like a free AI tool or GPT), not a lesson they have to study.

For each idea:
- What it is, in one line.
- The quick win my viewer gets.
- How it leads naturally to my one main offer (the next easy yes).
- How hard it is for me to make (easy / medium / hard).

Use my viewer avatar's problems and words. Recommend the best one to start with.
````
