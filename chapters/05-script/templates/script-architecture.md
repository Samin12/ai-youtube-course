## 1. Hook (first 5–30 seconds, written word for word)

*First sentence matches the title. First shot matches the thumbnail. (Aprilynne Alter) No "sup guys, hit like." (Jon Dorman)*

- **Promise:** is this what they clicked for?
- **Proof:** why should they listen to you? *(one line you reuse every video)*
- **Plan:** how are you getting them there?
- **Persona:** is this for them?
- **Effort (optional):** how much work went into this? *(input bias, Aprilynne Alter)*
