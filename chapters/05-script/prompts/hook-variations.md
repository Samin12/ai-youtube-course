# Hook variations prompt

The core prompt is from Nate Curtiss's course. The 4 P's check is from Jake Trinder.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude after your outline is done.

````text
Write three hook variations: bold claim, relatable pain, surprising fact.

Context:
- My title, thumbnail and outline are in my-video/ (or pasted below). Write in my voice from my-channel/brand-voice.md.
- Each hook is 30 seconds max when read out loud, written word for word.
- The first sentence must match the title.
- Each hook must hit the 4 P's. Label each part:
  - Promise: is this what I clicked for?
  - Proof: why should I listen to you?
  - Plan: how are you getting me there?
  - Persona: is this for me?
- No "hey guys" or "hit like" at the start.
- Only use proof that's true for me. If you don't know my proof, leave [my proof] for me to fill in.
````
