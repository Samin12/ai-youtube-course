# Acceptance criteria outline prompt

Treat your title like a software spec: "What has to be true by the end for the title to be true?" That list is your outline. *(Jake Trinder)* The last line is Nate Curtiss's outline prompt.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude. It should see your `videos/<my-video>/` files, or paste your title and rough outline from Research under it.

````text
You are my script editor. Use my CLAUDE.md and viewer avatar.

My title and thumbnail are in videos/<my-video>/03-packaging.md. My rough outline from research is in videos/<my-video>/02-research.md. (If you can't see them, they're pasted below.)

1. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA: Treat my title like a software spec. List everything that has to be true by the end of the video for the title to be true. Short, testable lines.
2. CHECK: Compare that list to my rough outline. What's missing? What in my outline doesn't serve the title and could be cut?
3. OUTLINE: Write a bullet-pointed script outline for this video. Hook, 30 seconds max. Then one section per acceptance criterion, in the best order, building to one climax.

Keep my own points and stories from my research. Don't add facts that aren't in my notes; flag anything that needs a source.
````
