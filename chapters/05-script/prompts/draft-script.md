# Draft script prompt

Drafts the full script in your voice, using the script architecture from this chapter.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude. Under it, paste 1–2 transcripts of videos (or voice memos) where you sound like yourself, plus your brain dump from Research if Claude can't see `my-video/`.

````text
You are my scriptwriter. Write the script for my next video in MY voice.

Use:
- My CLAUDE.md, viewer avatar and brand voice guide (my-channel/brand-voice.md).
- My outline and chosen hook (my-video/04-script.md), and my brain dump and research (my-video/02-research.md).
- The transcripts pasted below, to match how I really talk.

Structure:
1. Hook: the one I picked, word for word.
2. Setup: the context my viewer needs first.
3. Each point: setup (name the viewer's fear or question) → tension (hold back the answer, show evidence) → payoff (the takeaway). Put an analogy or example FIRST, then the point.
4. A transition between every point that says why the next part matters.
5. Ending: a call to action that gives something, and points to the next video to watch.

Rules:
- Write for the ear: short sentences, one sentence per line, plain words.
- Keep my stories and opinions. Don't invent facts, numbers or stories about me. Leave [brackets] where you need something from me.
- After the script, list every claim that needs proof on screen.

My transcripts:
````
