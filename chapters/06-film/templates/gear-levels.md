# Gear levels checklist

> Audio > lighting > camera. You don't need it all. Start at Level 1 and upgrade only when it's holding you back.

## Level 1: just start

- [ ] A good mic. This is the one thing worth buying. (I recommend the DJI Mic Mini.)
- [ ] Your phone or laptop camera
- [ ] A window for light (free)
