# Chapter 3: Research

**Stage 2 of 8:** Ideation → **Research** → Packaging → Script → Film → Edit → Repurpose → Publish

## Why this matters

Your decisions are only as good as your information. Economists call this bounded rationality: you can only choose as well as what you know. *(Jake Trinder)* Good research is what makes your video better than the 20 others on the same topic.

## What you'll learn

- **Brain dump first, so you don't sound like AI.** If you start with AI, you get the same generic take as everyone else. So first, write down your own experience, opinions and stories, "uncorrupted by research," so it stays "100% authentic." *(Marcus Jones)* Easiest way: a voice memo, then give the transcript to Claude. *(Nate Curtiss)*
- **Collect, then connect.** Gather sources first: videos, X/Twitter threads, articles, interviews, podcasts. Then sort every point into buckets. A bucket that several sources land in is a strong point. *(Aprilynne Alter)*
- **Study the best videos on the topic.** Watch the top videos all the way through and look for patterns. "Garbage in, garbage out." *(Marcus Jones)*
- **Study the flops too.** "If you only research winners, you can prove any bad idea." *(Jake Trinder)*
- **Find the gap.** What did everyone else miss? That's your reason to exist on this topic.
- **Make them feel smart.** "Viewers don't care about how smart you are, only how smart you make them feel." His simple version got 900,000 views after his in-depth version flopped. *(Ed Lawrence)*

## What you'll build

[`videos/<your-video>/02-research.md`](../../videos/_template/02-research.md): your brain dump, your sources, the buckets, the gap, and a rough outline.

## Steps

1. **Brain dump.** Record a voice memo with your own take on the idea. Get the transcript and run [`prompts/brain-dump.md`](prompts/brain-dump.md).
2. **Collect sources.** The top videos on your topic, a few flops, plus threads, articles and podcasts. Grab transcripts where you can. Timestamp your notes.
3. **Connect them.** Run [`prompts/collect-and-connect.md`](prompts/collect-and-connect.md) with your brain dump and sources pasted in. It sorts points into buckets, finds the gap, and drafts a rough outline.
4. **Save it** in `videos/<your-video>/02-research.md`. The rough outline is where the Script chapter starts.

**How I do it:** I brain dump first, then run my research skill, which pulls points from X/Twitter, YouTube and the web. I put it all in Poppy next to my brain dump and have AI sort it into buckets. *[Coming soon: my research skill]*

## Check it

- [ ] Is your own take still in there, or did AI flatten it?
- [ ] Does the outline have something the top videos don't?
- [ ] Is every point backed by a source or real experience?
- [ ] Would a beginner feel smart after watching, not lost?

## Tips

- **Timestamp your notes,** so you can find the clip later. *(Aprilynne Alter)*
- **Keep a swipe file** of great videos and threads. I keep mine in Poppy.
