# Collect and connect prompt

Sort your sources into buckets, find the gap, and draft a rough outline. *(Collect, then connect: Aprilynne Alter. Study the flops too: Jake Trinder.)*

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude. Under it, paste your brain dump, then your sources (transcripts, threads, articles, notes). Mark which videos did well and which flopped.

````text
You are my research assistant. Use my CLAUDE.md and viewer avatar. My video idea is in videos/<my-video>/01-idea.md (or below).

Below are my brain dump and my sources.

1. BUCKETS: Sort every useful point from the sources into buckets (topics). For each bucket, list which sources it came from. Put the buckets that several sources agree on first: those are strong points.
2. WINNERS VS FLOPS: What did the videos that did well have that the flops didn't? Only use what's in the sources.
3. THE GAP: What did everyone miss, or explain badly? Where does my brain dump say something the sources don't?
4. ROUGH OUTLINE: Turn the strongest buckets plus my own take into 5–8 points for the video, in order. Mark each point with its source, or "my experience".
5. Keep it simple enough that my viewer feels smart, not lost.

Only use facts that are in my sources or brain dump. If something needs checking, flag it.

My brain dump and sources:
````
