# Brain dump prompt

Get your own take down first, before any research, so your video doesn't sound like everyone else's. *(Marcus Jones; voice memo method from Nate Curtiss)*

**How to use it:** record a voice memo about your video idea. Say everything you think, know, and have lived about it. Get the transcript, then paste this prompt and the transcript into Claude.

````text
Below is a transcript of me talking about my next video idea (it's in videos/<my-video>/01-idea.md if you can see it).

Organize MY thoughts. Don't add research or new ideas yet.
1. List my main points, in my words.
2. List my stories and examples.
3. List my opinions, especially any that go against the usual advice.
4. List the questions I raised but didn't answer.
Quote my exact phrases where you can. Keep it short.

My transcript:
````
