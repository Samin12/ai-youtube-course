# Check my setup

Use this to make sure Claude can see your `CLAUDE.md`.

**How to use it:** open a new chat (in Claude Code, or in the Claude Project you made), paste the prompt below, and press Enter.

````text
Read my CLAUDE.md. Then tell me, in 3 short bullets:
1. Who I am and what I do, in your own words.
2. Which parts of my CLAUDE.md are still empty (still in [brackets]).
3. What I should fill in next.

If you can't see a CLAUDE.md at all, say so, and tell me in plain steps how to add it (Claude Code: open the repo folder; Claude chat: add the file to a Project or paste it in).
Keep it short and plain.
````
