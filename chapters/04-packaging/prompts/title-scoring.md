# Title scoring prompt

The core prompt is from Nate Curtiss's course. The extra checks come from this chapter.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude (it should see your `CLAUDE.md` and viewer avatar). Paste your own titles under it, if you have them.

````text
Write 10 title variations. Score each from 1 to 10 for click-worthiness. Recommend a top three.

Context:
- My video idea and research are in my-video/01-idea.md and my-video/02-research.md (or pasted below).
- Write for my viewer avatar, in their words.
- If I paste my own titles below, score those too.
- Keep titles under about 50 characters.
- For each title, name the click emotion it uses: curiosity, fear, or desire.
- Only promise what the video can actually deliver.

My titles (optional):
````
