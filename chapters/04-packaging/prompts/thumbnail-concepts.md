# Thumbnail concepts prompt

The core prompt is from Nate Curtiss's course. The extra checks come from this chapter.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude after you've picked your title.

````text
Suggest three thumbnail concepts: composition, emotion, three-word text max, color palette.

Context:
- My title is: [paste your title]
- Make the title and thumbnail tell ONE idea together. The title makes them interested; the thumbnail adds intrigue.
- Don't repeat the title in the thumbnail text.
- One main subject per thumbnail. It has to stand out in a feed and make sense at phone size in 2 seconds.
- For each concept, say which scroll stopper it uses: a face, money or big numbers, emotion, or bright color.
````
