# Make the posts prompt

Turns the ideas from your video into posts for each platform.

**How to use it:** run this after [`find-the-ideas.md`](find-the-ideas.md), in the same chat. Tell it which ideas to use.

````text
Turn these ideas into posts: [list the idea numbers you picked].

For each idea, write:
1. A short-form video script (for Shorts, Reels or TikTok): a strong first line, one point, short and spoken.
2. An X/Twitter thread: a hook post, then a few short posts.
3. A carousel for Instagram and LinkedIn: one short line per slide.

Rules:
- Write in my voice (my-channel/brand-voice.md).
- Each piece must make sense on its own, without the video.
- End each piece by pointing to the full video or my offer (use the link from my-channel/offer.md).
- Only use what's in my video. Don't add new claims.
````
