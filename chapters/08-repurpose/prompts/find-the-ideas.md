# Find the ideas prompt

Pulls the separate ideas out of your finished video, so each one can become its own post.

**How to use it:** paste this into Claude with your finished video's transcript under it.

````text
Below is the transcript of my finished YouTube video. Use my CLAUDE.md and viewer avatar.

Pull out every separate idea inside it that could stand on its own as a post.
For each idea give me:
- A one-line summary.
- The best quote from the transcript, word for word, with its timestamp if there is one.
- The best format for it: short clip, short-form script, X/Twitter thread, carousel, or newsletter section.
- Why my viewer would care, in one line.

Rank them, best first. Only use what's in the transcript.

My transcript:
````
